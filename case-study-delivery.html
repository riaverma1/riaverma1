Background

The problem with how deliveries were being planned

[EDIT: Describe the baseline state here. e.g. "Delivery routes and DC-to-store assignments were largely static — set years earlier and never re-optimized as the network grew. Planners were making manual adjustments in spreadsheets, often with incomplete visibility into total cost."]

The result was predictable: suboptimal DC assignments, redundant miles driven, and no systematic way to ask "what's the cheapest way to serve this store given today's constraints?" The opportunity was there — it just needed to be modeled.

The core question

"Given our network of 75 distribution centers and stores, how should we assign each store to a DC — and what delivery schedule — to minimize total cost while meeting all service constraints?"
